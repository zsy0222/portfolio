# Travel wall surface

`paper-grain.webp` is an AI-generated decorative cotton-paper material, created with the built-in image generation tool and converted to a 768 × 768 WebP (81,456 bytes). It depicts no destination or personal travel event.

Prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square tileable website background material. Primary request: a subtle realistic archival cotton paper surface, photographed perfectly flat from directly overhead. Warm off-white with a faint linen-gray cast, tiny fine cotton fibers, barely visible natural shallow creases and gentle paper tooth. Even diffuse illumination from all directions, consistent tone from edge to edge, seamless repeat-friendly surface. The visual contrast must be very low so dark or light website text stays readable over it. Dry matte paper, refined museum archival stationery, natural and tactile, no plastic or glossy texture. Fill the complete square with the paper surface. No objects, no letters, no handwriting, no shapes, no borders, no holes, no stains, no worn or torn edges, no shadows, no vignette, no gradient lighting, no watermark. 1024 by 1024 square composition.

Travel posters and illustrated souvenir tickets are retained unchanged from the user-selected `codex/travel-wall-archive` branch, commit `7b3f5d845a9191f95f361b7df0ec92053e3fa690`. Their source files remain under `public/images/v2/travel-wall/`. These artworks are not presented as documentary photographs. Place metadata is retained from that branch; ambiguous artwork landmark labels are not used as new trip claims.
